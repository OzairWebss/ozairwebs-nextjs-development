# Instructions for automated contributors

Read README.md and CONTRIBUTING.md before suggesting changes. Preserve the distinction between official documentation, public business statements and illustrative recommendations. Use primary sources for technical claims and record the date/version checked.

Never invent results, testimonials, qualifications or first-hand experience. Do not add private client code, credentials or account data. Avoid mass-generated pages and promotional link repetition. Keep prose useful without a commercial click. Check relative links after moving files and identify conceptual examples accurately. Do not claim human authorship or editorial approval on a person's behalf.

Document proposed changes and evidence for review. This file guides contributions; it is not a search-ranking mechanism or permission to publish externally.
