# Discussion standards

Keep discussion specific, respectful and supported by evidence. Critique a recommendation or example without attacking its author. Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, personal-data exposure and promotional spam are not acceptable.

Do not post customer data, private account information or confidential project material. Describe a problem using a minimal, anonymous example where possible. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict repeated abuse. Concerns can be reported to info@ozairwebs.com; report only information needed to explain the issue. These standards apply to this repository's issues and pull requests.
